# Sample QA Results

These results demonstrate how the AI Customer Success QA framework can evaluate customer interactions and provide coaching recommendations.

---

## Scenario 1: Delayed Refund

### Overall QA Score

**78%**

### Category Scores

| Category | Score | Weight | Reasoning |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Empathy & Active Listening | 3/5 | 15% | The agent acknowledged the customer but did not directly address their frustration about the delay. |
| Communication | 5/5 | 15% | The agent provided a clear and easy-to-understand explanation of the refund timeline. |
| Issue Identification | 4/5 | 20% | The agent identified that the refund had already been processed. |
| Resolution & Next Steps | 4/5 | 20% | The agent provided a clear timeframe for when the refund should arrive. |
| Ownership | 3/5 | 15% | The agent provided information but could have demonstrated stronger ownership by offering additional assistance if the refund did not arrive. |
| Professionalism | 5/5 | 15% | The agent remained professional and respectful throughout the interaction. |

### Key Strengths

- Provided accurate and clear information.
- Explained the expected refund timeframe.
- Maintained a professional tone.

### Areas for Improvement

- Acknowledge the customer's frustration more directly.
- Demonstrate stronger ownership of the issue.
- Offer a clear follow-up action if the refund does not arrive within the expected timeframe.

### Coaching Recommendation

Before providing the refund timeline, acknowledge the customer's frustration and reassure them that you will help them understand what is happening.

### Improved Response Example

"I understand how frustrating it is to wait for your refund, especially after already contacting us. I checked your account and can confirm that the refund was processed yesterday. It normally takes 5–7 business days to appear in your account. If you don't receive it within that timeframe, please let us know and we'll be happy to investigate it further."

## Scenario 2: Account Access Issue

### Overall QA Score

**96%**

### Category Scores

| Category | Score | Weight | Reasoning |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Empathy & Active Listening | 5/5 | 15% | The agent acknowledged the customer's difficulty and showed willingness to help. |
| Communication | 5/5 | 15% | The agent communicated clearly and explained what information was needed. |
| Issue Identification | 5/5 | 20% | The agent identified that the issue was related to the missing password reset email. |
| Resolution & Next Steps | 5/5 | 20% | The agent provided a clear next step by escalating the issue to the technical team. |
| Ownership | 5/5 | 15% | The agent took responsibility and documented the customer's previous troubleshooting steps. |
| Professionalism | 4/5 | 15% | The interaction was professional and respectful, with only minor opportunity to make the response more concise. |

### Key Strengths

- Demonstrated empathy.
- Identified the customer's issue.
- Took ownership of the escalation.
- Prevented the customer from having to repeat previous troubleshooting steps.
- Clearly explained the next step.

### Areas for Improvement

- Provide a more specific expected timeframe for the technical team's response if available.

### Coaching Recommendation

Continue demonstrating ownership by clearly explaining what will happen next and setting an expectation for follow-up whenever possible.

### Improved Response Example

"Thank you for confirming that. I've verified that your account exists, and it appears the reset emails are being delayed. I'll escalate this to our technical team and include the troubleshooting you've already completed so you won't have to repeat those steps. I'll also make sure you receive an update as soon as we have more information."

## Scenario 3: Product Complaint

### Overall QA Score

**91%**

### Category Scores

| Category | Score | Weight | Reasoning |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Empathy & Active Listening | 5/5 | 15% | The agent recognized the customer's frustration and acknowledged the urgency. |
| Communication | 5/5 | 15% | The response was clear, professional, and easy to understand. |
| Issue Identification | 5/5 | 20% | The agent understood that the product arrived damaged and that the customer needed a quick solution. |
| Resolution & Next Steps | 4/5 | 20% | The agent provided clear next steps but did not immediately confirm whether a replacement could arrive before the event. |
| Ownership | 4/5 | 15% | The agent showed willingness to help but could demonstrate stronger ownership by proactively checking urgent replacement options. |
| Professionalism | 5/5 | 15% | The agent remained professional and customer-focused. |

### Key Strengths

- Demonstrated empathy.
- Recognized the urgency of the situation.
- Provided clear next steps.
- Maintained a professional and helpful tone.

### Areas for Improvement

- Investigate expedited replacement options because of the customer's deadline.
- Provide a clearer expectation for when the customer will receive an update.

### Coaching Recommendation

When a customer communicates a specific deadline, identify whether there is an opportunity to provide an expedited solution rather than following only the standard process.

### Improved Response Example

"I'm sorry the product arrived damaged, especially since you need it for an event tomorrow. Please send me a photo of the damage and your order number. I'll review the replacement options and check whether we can arrange an expedited replacement. I'll make sure you receive an update as quickly as possible."

## Key Takeaways

The examples demonstrate how the framework can be used to:

- Measure customer interaction quality consistently.
- Identify specific strengths and weaknesses.
- Recognize opportunities for stronger ownership.
- Turn QA findings into actionable coaching.
- Connect customer experience with agent development.
